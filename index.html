How it works

From structure to finished site

  1. Pick a structure

    Choose the layout closest to the idea: a stay, a service, a product, an app or a dashboard.

  2. Pour in the content

    Replace the placeholder words, photos, videos and numbers. The layout stays the same.

  3. Check it

    Every build is checked on phone and laptop and scanned for keys and private details.

  4. Publish

    Only the finished output goes public, and only when you say so.

Blocks

Built from one set of blocks

Heroes with photo or video and a quick form, facts strips, intros, card grids, galleries, maps, searchable lists, Q&A, booking forms with a summary, pricing, app bars, tabs, tickers, number tiles, ranked tables, charts, alerts and phone screens. Every structure uses the same parts, so they all look and behave alike.

FAQ

Questions

Why is there no real content?

On purpose. Each structure is a clean starting point, so any business can use it without editing out someone else's words.

Where do the photos and videos come from?

Free stock from Pexels, used as stand-ins. Replace them with your own.

Can a structure be changed?

Yes. Blocks can be added, removed or reordered. The structure is where you start, not a limit.

Do they work on phones?

Every structure is built phone-first and checked at phone and laptop widths before it is published.
