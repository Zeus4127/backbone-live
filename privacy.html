Privacy

Last updated 16 November 2026.

What this site collects

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. The forms on the structure pages are templates and send nothing.

What stays on your device

One setting: whether "Block names" is switched on. It is stored in your browser only, and you can clear it at any time.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages, which receives your IP address to deliver the pages, like any website host.

Questions

Ask on the project's GitHub page.
