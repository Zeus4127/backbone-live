Privacy

Last updated 16 November 2026. This covers the Backbone Studio app and this website.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies, in the app or on this site.

What stays on your phone

Your ideas, saved projects, watch list and alert settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them.

What the app downloads

The app reads one public data file (live.json) about every 15 minutes to show prices and send alerts. Like any web request, the server that hosts the file (GitHub Pages) receives your device's IP address to deliver it. Nothing else is sent.

Permissions

  • Internet: to read the public data file.
  • Notifications: to show your price alerts. You can turn them off at any time.
  • Battery setting (optional): lets the 15-minute check keep running in the background.

Market data

Prices are information only, not financial advice.
